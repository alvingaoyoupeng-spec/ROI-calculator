Valuation Methodology & Calculation Glossary

1. Gross Revenue (Monthly) Formula:
Gross Revenue = Daily Foot Traffic × Traffic Conversion Rate × Average Item Price × Items Per Order × 30 Days

2. Net Profit (Monthly) Formula:
Net Profit = Gross Revenue × Profit Margin − Platform Fee − Transaction Fee − Cellular Data Fee − POS Device Rental Fee

3. Payback Period Formula:
Payback Period = (Product Price × Device Units) / Net Profit
※Notes:
①This ROI calculation model does not account for labor or operational costs (e.g., driver wages, fuel, and restocking fees) and is for reference only.
②Actual ROI is driven by multiple variables, including location foot traffic, conversion rates, average order value (AOV), and product net profit margins.
